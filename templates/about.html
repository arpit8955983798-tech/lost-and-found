About LostLink

An intelligent Lost and Found Management System designed to make finding misplaced belongings easier, faster, and more efficient.

What is LostLink?

LostLink is a web-based intelligent Lost and Found Management System designed to help students, institutions, and shared communities report, search, and recover misplaced belongings more efficiently.

The Problem

Traditional lost-and-found processes generally depend on manual notices, verbal communication, or simple keyword searches. This can make it difficult to identify a matching item when lost and found reports use different descriptions.

Our Solution

LostLink addresses this problem by combining a centralized database with Natural Language Processing (NLP)-based similarity matching.

Users can submit detailed reports for lost or found items, including item name, category, description, colour, brand, location, and date.

Intelligent Matching

When a report is submitted, the system compares it with relevant opposite-type reports. Text descriptions are converted into TF-IDF vectors and compared using cosine similarity.

Additional similarity scores for location, date, category, and colour are combined through a weighted scoring mechanism to produce an overall possible-match percentage.

Technologies Used: Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and Scikit-learn.
